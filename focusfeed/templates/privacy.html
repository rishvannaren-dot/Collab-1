Privacy

Draft for the project team. Have it reviewed before real users sign up.

What we store

  • Your username and a hashed password (we never store the password itself).
  • Your interests, grade, and goal, so we can build your feed.
  • Saved YouTube search results, which are not linked to you.

What we don't do

  • We don't track what you watch.
  • We don't sell or share your data.

Under 18

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account. They can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time.

Videos

Videos play on YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.
