Privacy

Draft for the project team. Before real users sign up, add a contact email and have this page and the sign-up consent reviewed by someone who knows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

What we store

  • Your username and a hashed password (we never store the password itself).
  • A hashed recovery key, if you make one (we never store the key itself).
  • When you made your account, and when you agreed to these notes (and which version).
  • Your interests, grade, and goal, so we can build your study cards and videos.
  • Your study plan: which chapter you're on in each subject, and the day you started it.
  • Topics you mark as studied, how sure you felt, and the dates (no times). They are used only for your progress and revision, never shared, and removed when you undo a mark or delete your account.
  • Failed sign-in attempts (the username and the time), to stop password guessing. They are deleted after 15 minutes or when you sign in.
  • Saved YouTube search results and Wikipedia summaries, which are shared by everyone and not linked to you.

Cookies and your device

  • One cookie keeps you signed in and protects your forms from other websites. It ends when you log out or close your browser, or after 30 days if you ticked "Keep me signed in on this device". There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Your light or dark mode choice is saved in your browser only.

Other services

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts on every page, so Google can see your IP address and browser.
  • On the Videos page, thumbnails load from YouTube, which can see the same.
  • When live search is on, our server sends YouTube search phrases such as "Electrostatics Gauss's law Class 12 JEE". Your username is never included.
  • For the short explanations, our server looks up topic names such as "Coulomb's law" on Wikipedia. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Videos and search links open on YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't track which videos you open.
  • We don't show ads, use analytics, or sell or share your data.

Download or delete your data

  • On your Account page, Download my data gives you everything stored about you as a file.
  • Delete my account, on the same page, removes your account and everything stored about you straight away.

How long we keep things

  • Failed sign-in attempts: 15 minutes.
  • Your account and what you've saved: until you delete them.
  • Backups made by the team are kept for 7 days, so a deleted account is gone from them within a week.

Under 18

If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree before you create an account. You can delete your account yourself at any time. A parent or guardian who doesn't have your password can ask the Focus Feed team to delete it.
